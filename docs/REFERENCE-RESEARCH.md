# Reference research

The four repositories were inspected read-only. No reference project was modified or deployed.

- **DigitalBurjFinalMain** — service ecosystem, static-first SEO architecture, practical editorial content and clear enquiry routes. The new site retains useful topic coverage and legacy redirects while replacing every page template and visual system.
- **Loadbyton OS** — operational context, enquiry-to-collection workflows, finance linked to work, tenant and role boundaries. These informed the connected-workspace demo and the practical service descriptions. The portfolio story follows the README’s stated scope without claiming live-provider acceptance.
- **Project Loadbyton** — marketplace journeys, simulated operational UI and making a complex handover understandable. These informed the new interactive previews and the Loadbyton portfolio interface study.
- **Elvora Shipping** — service discovery, practical tools, honest channel handovers and clear limits. These informed the guided service finder, enquiry form and simple process narrative.

Project cards are new HTML interface studies with sample records, not live screenshots or customer results. Source links lead to the user-supplied repositories. Geographic positioning and regional finance assumptions from the reference projects are not carried into this website.

Brand authority: the user-uploaded **The-Collective-Master-Brand-Assets.zip**, including the approved master, compact logo, symbol, reversed artwork, six division identities and palette tokens. Documents were read as design reference; stationery placeholders were not treated as configured accounts or instructions.
