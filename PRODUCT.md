# the collective

A worldwide-facing marketing website for a connected technology and creative practice. The brand is the supplied lowercase `the collective`, with the tagline **Intelligent Management & Automation Matrix** and canonical domain `theimamcollective.com`.

The audience is a business team looking to improve its operations, automate a repeated workflow, build a digital product, reach customers or develop capability. The primary action is a useful project conversation, not an account or product checkout.

Capabilities: Collective OS, AI, Studio, Growth, Academy and Talent. The website includes six substantive service pages, four supplied portfolio project stories, industry applications, company approach, USD pricing, contact and project briefs, practical field notes, a privacy notice and local interactive demos. Academy, Talent and Jobs describe services; no authenticated LMS, candidate directory or recruitment platform is claimed.

The user requested a complete visual and content rebuild from the new master brand assets, drawing inspiration from DigitalBurj, Loadbyton OS, Project Loadbyton and Elvora Shipping. The previous regional identity and geographic positioning are retired. Portfolio projects are owner-supplied work, with scope grounded in the public repositories. No customer metrics, testimonials, global offices or production claims are invented.

Published price figures retain indicative platform rates in USD from the reference catalogue. They are identified as estimates, with implementation, usage and taxes separate. The annual display is twelve times the monthly reference, with no implied discount. Project services require an agreed quotation.

Public emails and phone are user-provided and retained as international contact channels. Existing email delivery integration is retained, with the new brand sender. Automatic delivery requires a configured Resend binding and verified sender. Provider failure never appears as a successful submission; the visitor chooses whether to open an unsent email draft.
